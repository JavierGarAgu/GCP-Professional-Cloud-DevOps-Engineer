# SRE Reliability and Deployment Velocity

## Exam Question

A service has met its Service Level Objective (SLO) for the last six months. Customers are happy, most operations are automated, and there are very few manual tasks.

What should you do?

**Correct Answers:** **B** and **C**

## Why B is Correct

The service is already very reliable.

This means you can increase deployment velocity or accept a little more risk.

You have enough reliability, so you can focus on delivering new changes faster.

## Why C is Correct

The service does not need much engineering work because it is stable.

It is better to move engineers to other services that need more reliability.

This helps the company use engineering time more efficiently.

## Why the Other Answers Are Wrong

**A.** Incorrect.

The current SLO already meets the business needs. There is no reason to make it stricter.

**D.** Incorrect.

Reliability is already good enough. The team does not need to spend more time improving it.

**E.** Incorrect.

There is no evidence that the current SLIs are missing important information. The service is already meeting its objectives.

## Key SRE Concept

When a service is reliable, customers are satisfied, and operations are automated:

* Increase deployment velocity.
* Accept a little more risk if needed.
* Move engineering effort to services that need more reliability.
* Keep a balance between reliability, development speed, and business needs.
